Add three-condition ablation, live demonstration dashboard, and Week 8 analysis

Experiments (560 live runs this week; 848 total across the project):
- Live counterfactual ablation: the 80 proposals the gate rejected, applied
  verbatim to the live lab. 32 broke intent, 6 breached policy, 24 broke
  connectivity. Converts the Week 7 post-hoc claim into measurement.
- Free-form ungoverned baseline: 120 runs across 12 models emitting raw router
  commands. 40 policy breaches (33%), 53 broke intent, 4 runs left the lab
  unrecoverable by any reconfiguration command.
- Three-condition comparison: breach rate 0% (guarded, n=360) -> 8% (schema
  only, n=80) -> 33% (free-form, n=120).

Findings:
- The schema alone gives a fourfold breach reduction with no gate; the gate and
  approval layer remove the remainder. Neither layer is redundant.
- A configuration-fact check (C1) caught a relocated security control that
  reachability testing (R2) passed - direct support for the two-part validator.
- All 12 models breached the policy at least once when ungoverned.
- Damage concentrates entirely in requests where the correct answer is refusal:
  legitimate requests produced 2 damaged runs out of 48.
- Gate confusion matrix: recall 1.000, 0 false negatives over 156 gated runs.
